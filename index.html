H.264 MP4/MOV parses natively. Incompatible H.264 pairs are normalized with WebCodecs when available, otherwise ffmpeg.wasm. Normalization re-encodes video-only H.264 at 30 fps with a closed 30-frame GOP, an IDR at frame 0, no B-frames, and a common larger letterboxed frame. Other containers and codecs use the lazily loaded ffmpeg.wasm fallback. Conversion is local; large files can exceed device memory or fail on iOS Safari, and hardware acceleration is not guaranteed. Merged exports still have no audio.
